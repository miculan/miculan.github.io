title: Lecturing at the AILA Summer School of Logic 2026
---
Thrilled to be a lecturer at the [Summer School of Logic](https://www.ailalogica.it/attivita/scuola-estiva/come-partecipare-alla-scuola-estiva-di-logica/) of the [Associazione Italiana di Logica e sue Applicazioni](https://www.ailalogica.it)! The school is held at the Villaggio Olimpico of Bardonecchia, in the Italian Alps, from August 31 to September 5, 2026.
My course is on _Proof Theory, Type Theory, Proof Assistants and AI_, covering the path from the theoretical foundations (typed λ-calculus, dependent types, the λ-cube) to modern proof assistants and their integration with AI. Slides and Lean exercises are available in the [course repository](https://github.com/miculan/aila2026-lean).
It is always exciting discussing with students and fellow researchers!
